Rules for Adega Ribeira

Wine and spirits · about 310 deliveries a week

Proof required

A delivery only counts as done when the driver has all of these.

The parcel at the door or in the recipient’s hands, with the label readable.

The recipient signs on the driver’s phone. Parcels can’t be left in a safe place.

For items the sender marks 18+. The driver checks the recipient’s ID and records its type, never its number. Without valid ID, the parcel goes back to the depot.

Applies to deliveries scheduled from this date. The earliest is tomorrow, as routes are planned the day before.
