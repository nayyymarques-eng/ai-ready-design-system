# Checkbox

A yes or no choice inside a form, applied when the form is saved.

## Markup
```html
<label class="checkbox"><input type="checkbox"> Label</label>
```

## Variants and states
None.

## Use for
- A yes or no choice saved with a form

## Never
- A setting that applies at once (use a switch)

## Lives in
Forms.

## Tokens
--s2

## Checked by
FIELD-01
