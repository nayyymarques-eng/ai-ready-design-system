Rules for Corvo Wine Club

Drivers must collect every proof ticked here before they can mark a Corvo Wine Club parcel as delivered.

Applies to wine, spirits and gift sets with alcohol. Drivers check a photo ID and enter its last four digits.

Drivers never leave age-restricted items with a neighbour or in a safe place.

Start date

Parcels loaded on or after this date use the new rules. Until then, drivers collect a photo and a signature, with no ID check.
